# Art prompt: Short 17 (Thanksgiving, fourth Thursday)

ChatGPT in Chrome (chatgpt.com), one image per message in one new chat; a light model setting (not Pro). Composition guides only: they get traced and re-inked. Ask for minimal sketches from the start. us-map is reused from Short 16.

Paint: red lead throughout (no colours named in the narration). The calendar is the motif: it opens and closes the Short (loop), and the paint lands on its Thursday squares.

## Style (send once first)

I'm going to ask you for 12 drawings, one per message. Use this style for every one:
- A minimal pen sketch on plain white: about 25-40 lines in total.
- Black ink, one line weight, slightly wobbly, like a quick sketch in a notebook.
- Only the main subject's outline and a few inner lines. No background, no shading, no hatching, no fill, no color, no gray, no text, letters, numbers or logos.
- Lots of empty white paper. Subject centered, about 70% of the frame. Square image.
- People seen from behind or from the side, with simple faces at most.
- Not clip art, not vector: it should look drawn by a person.
