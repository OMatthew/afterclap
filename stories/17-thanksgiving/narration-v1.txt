Why is Thanksgiving on the fourth Thursday of November?
It used to be the last Thursday. Abraham Lincoln picked it, and presidents kept it for decades.
[[PAUSE 0.4]]
Then came 1939. That November had five Thursdays, so Thanksgiving would land on the very last day of the month.
Store owners were worried. A late Thanksgiving meant fewer shopping days before Christmas, in the middle of the Great Depression.
So they asked President Franklin Roosevelt to move it up a week. He did.
[[PAUSE 0.4]]
The country split. Some states followed him. Others kept the old date. Texas took both days off.
Critics called it "Franksgiving." Families in neighboring states sat down to turkey a week apart.
[[PAUSE 0.4]]
Did the extra week help the stores? A government survey later found no real change in sales.
In 1941, Congress ended the confusion. Thanksgiving would be the fourth Thursday of November, even when there's a fifth.
Most years, that's the same day. That's why hardly anyone notices.
