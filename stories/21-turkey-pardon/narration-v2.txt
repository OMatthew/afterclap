The first time a president talked about pardoning a turkey, he was dodging a question about a scandal.

[[PAUSE 0.3]]

So why does every president pardon one now?
Since 1947, turkey farmers have brought the president a live bird. The early ones were meant for dinner.
But some presidents spared theirs. Kennedy sent his back to the farm.
No president called it a pardon. Not until 1987.
That year, reporters pressed Ronald Reagan: would he pardon two aides from the Iran-Contra scandal? He wouldn't say.
But the turkey, he joked, he would have pardoned.
Two years later, George Bush said it with no joke: this turkey was pardoned. Every president since has done the same.

[[PAUSE 0.5]]

It isn't a real pardon. The turkey hasn't broken any law.
And it was bred for the table, so many live only a year or two more.
