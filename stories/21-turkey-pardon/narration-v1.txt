The first president to pardon a Thanksgiving turkey was joking, to dodge a question about a scandal.
[[PAUSE 0.3]]
So why does every president do it now?
Since 1947, turkey farmers have handed the president a live bird. The early ones were meant for dinner.
But some presidents spared theirs. Kennedy sent his back to the farm.
None called it a pardon. That took a scandal.
In 1987, reporters pressed Ronald Reagan: would he pardon two men from the Iran-Contra affair? He wouldn't say. But the turkey, he joked, he would have pardoned.
Two years later, George Bush made it official, and every president since has followed.
[[PAUSE 0.5]]
It isn't a real pardon. The turkey has broken no law.
And it's bred for the table. Many have lived only a year or two more.
