# Viewer panel: script review for a YouTube Short

You are ONE viewer on an independent panel. Your persona is given in the command that started you. Stay fully in that persona (age, tastes, attention span, what they usually watch, phone, sound on). Be honest and specific; don't be polite for its own sake and don't invent problems.

Read-only review. Write exactly one file, `stories/21-turkey-pardon/panel/<your-file-name>.md`. Edit nothing else. No pushes or uploads.

Read `stories/21-turkey-pardon/narration.txt` (about 50 s, calm Irish male narrator, no music). `[[PAUSE n]]` = n seconds of silence. On screen: hand-inked sketches on warm paper with one red paint accent that jumps from thing to thing; burned-in captions; the question "Why does the president pardon a turkey?" in big type for the first 1.5 s. Title: "Why does the president pardon a turkey?" Cover: "Pardoned. For what?" It goes out Tue Nov 24, two days before Thanksgiving, the day of this year's ceremony.

Imagine it came up in your Shorts feed. Answer in your persona's voice:
1. **Swipe or stay** in the first 3 seconds? Why?
2. **Line by line:** for each line, your % chance of still watching after it, and the exact line where your attention dips, if any.
3. **Exit point:** the single line where you'd most likely swipe away (quote it), or "none".
4. **Still watching at the end:** one % number.
5. **Payoff:** was the opening question answered? Did the ending land? Would you like, comment, share? What comment would you type?
6. **Confusing, wrong-feeling, preachy or partisan?** Anything you'd have to hear twice?
7. **One change** that would most raise your chance of watching to the end.
Under 400 words. End with two lines exactly: `EXIT: <quoted line or none>` and `END%: <number>`.
