The first time a president talked about pardoning a turkey, he was dodging a question about a scandal.

[[PAUSE 0.3]]

So why does every president pardon one now?
It was 1987. Reporters asked Ronald Reagan if he'd pardon two of his former aides, caught up in a scandal called Iran-Contra. He wouldn't say.
Instead, he joked that he would have pardoned the Thanksgiving turkey he'd just been given.
In 1989, the next president, George Bush, went ahead and did it. He told the crowd: this turkey is pardoned. Every president since has done the same.

[[PAUSE 0.5]]

Legally, there's nothing to pardon. The turkey hasn't broken any law.
And the birds are bred to be eaten, so many have lived only a year or two more.
