The first time a president talked about pardoning a turkey, he was dodging a question about a scandal.

[[PAUSE 0.3]]

So why does every president pardon one now?
It was 1987. Reporters pressed Ronald Reagan: would he pardon two former aides from a scandal called Iran-Contra? He wouldn't say.
Instead, he joked that he would have pardoned the turkey.
That turkey was a gift. Since 1947, turkey farmers have brought the president a live bird, and the early ones were meant for dinner.
Kennedy sent his back to the farm. But no president had called it a pardon.
Two years later, George Bush went ahead and did it. He told the crowd: this turkey is pardoned. Every president since has done the same.

[[PAUSE 0.5]]

The birds are bred to be eaten, so many have lived only a year or two more.
And legally, there's nothing to pardon. The turkey hasn't broken any law.
