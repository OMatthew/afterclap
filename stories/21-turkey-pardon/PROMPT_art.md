# Art prompt: Short 21 (turkey pardon)

ChatGPT in Chrome (chatgpt.com), one new chat, one image per message, default model (6.2), not Pro. Composition guides: they get traced and re-inked. Hook test: a face in the first scene, big shapes the paint can fill (the turkeys' bodies, never their heads).

Real presidents are drawn as simple sketches, side or three-quarter views, never photoreal, never caricature.

## Style (send once first)

I'm going to ask you for 10 drawings, one per message. Use this style for every one:
- A minimal pen sketch on plain white: about 25-45 lines in total.
- Black ink, one line weight, slightly wobbly, like a quick sketch in a notebook.
- Only outlines and a few inner lines. No background, no shading, no hatching, no fill, no color, no gray, no text, letters, numbers or logos.
- Lots of empty white paper. Subject centered, about 70% of the frame. Square image.
- Simple, friendly faces (dots for eyes, a short line for a mouth) are fine. Real people are simple sketches, never caricatures.
- Every turkey is a plain, big, white farm turkey (a broad white body), drawn alive and friendly unless I say roast.
- Not clip art, not vector: it should look drawn by a person.

## Items (file name: description)

1. podium-turkey: An older man in a suit at an outdoor lectern, three-quarter view, eyebrows raised and a sly half smile, glancing sideways at a big live white turkey standing on a small table beside him. His face clearly visible.
2. crate-steps: Two farmers in 1940s coats and caps carrying a big live white turkey between them up a few wide steps toward a tall columned doorway.
3. roast-dinner: A whole roast turkey on an oval platter on a bare table, with a folded napkin beside it. No knife.
4. farm-truck: A small 1960s pickup truck driving away along a country road toward a little barn, a live white turkey looking out over the tailgate.
5. reporters: Four reporters in 1980s suits crowding forward with handheld microphones and notepads, mouths open asking questions, seen from the side.
6. president-joke: The same older man as drawing 1, side view, laughing with one hand open toward the live white turkey on the table, as if telling a joke.
7. hand-over-turkey: A man in a suit, side view, holding one hand out flat over a big live white turkey standing on a table, as if granting it something; the turkey looks up at him.
8. row-of-lecterns: Five empty outdoor lecterns in a row, receding into the distance, each with a live white turkey on a small table beside it.
9. law-book: A big live white turkey peering curiously at a thick open law book lying on the ground.
10. straw-pen: A big heavy white turkey sitting in straw inside a simple wooden pen, a small barn behind, calm and sleepy.

## Paint plan (for the cut)
Red lead on: the lectern (1), the doorway (2), the platter rim (3), the truck (4), a microphone (5), the table (6), the turkey's body (7, 9: big fill), the row of turkeys' tables (8), the straw (10). Never on a turkey's head or wattle, never on a face or near a mouth. No colors named in the narration, so red throughout.
