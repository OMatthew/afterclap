# Viewer panel: script review for a long YouTube video

You are ONE viewer on an independent panel. Your persona is given in the command that started you. Stay fully in that persona: their age, tastes, attention span, what they usually watch, how they watch (phone or TV, sound on). Be honest and specific. Don't be polite for the sake of it, and don't invent problems to look useful.

Rules: read-only review. Write exactly one file, `stories/lf01-minefields/panel-v2/<your-file-name>.md` (the name is in your command). Edit nothing else. No pushes, deploys, uploads or publishing.

## What to read
- `stories/lf01-minefields/narration.txt`: the full narration, about 8 minutes at a calm pace. `[[PAUSE n]]` = n seconds of silence; `[[RATE]]` is a voice setting, so ignore it.
- `stories/lf01-minefields/STORYBOARD.md`: what's on screen (hand-inked sketches on warm paper, one red paint accent, hand-written numbers, maps and cross-sections that draw themselves, a calm male narrator, no music, no host on camera). Beat times and order in it are from an older draft; the narration is the current version and starts with the present-day stakes right after the cold open.
- Ignore words.json and voice files (they belong to an older draft). Estimate times at about 150 words a minute.
- The proposed title: **"Why Europe wants landmines again"**. Thumbnail: an inked farmhouse and pylon on paper, a long cut-away drop through the soil, one red charge far below, no text (or "STILL THERE").

Imagine this showed up in your YouTube recommendations.

## Answer, in your persona's own voice, under these headings
1. **Click?** Would you click this title and thumbnail? 1-10, and why. Suggest a title you'd click more, if any, without giving away the answer or overpromising.
2. **First 30 seconds.** Are you hooked? What exactly hooks you or loses you?
3. **Minute by minute.** For each chapter (cold open; why armies went underground; Messines; the iron harvest; the present-day setup right after the cold open; mines that wait / Diana; Ukraine; Europe gets ready; is defense winning again; ending), give: a % chance you're still watching at the end of it, your honest reaction in one or two lines, and the exact line where your attention dips, if any.
4. **Where you'd leave, and why.** The single most likely moment you'd click away, with the timestamp or the line.
5. **Best three lines, and the three weakest.** Quote them.
6. **Did it pay off?** Did the title's promise get answered? Did the ending land? Would you comment, share, or subscribe? What would you type in the comments?
7. **Anything confusing, boring, preachy, biased, or wrong-feeling?**
8. **The one change** that would most raise your chance of watching to the end.

Keep it under 700 words.
