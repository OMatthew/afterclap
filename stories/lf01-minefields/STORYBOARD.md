**Title (no payoff):** Why Finland wants a million landmines (panel v2: 4 of 5 preferred it; alt "Why Europe wants landmines again")
**Script:** draft 4 after two viewer panels (panel-v1, panel-v2) and the Fable review. Beat order now: cold open, present-day stakes, 1914, Messines, iron harvest, mines that wait, Diana, Ukraine, Europe, is defense winning, ending. Beat times below are from draft 1 and get recomputed from words.json.
**Alt:** Lightning set off a bomb from 1917. Five more are still buried.
**Thumbnail:** inked farmhouse and pylon on the paper; a long cut-away drop through the soil; one red charge far below. No text, or "STILL THERE".
**Format:** 16:9 landscape (engine `style.frame: 'landscape'`), about 8 min, captions off (SRT uploaded), chapters on.
**Voice:** Darren, RATE 150 (slower than Shorts).

## What's new compared with our Shorts (pushing the look)
- **Cross-sections as the spine.** The paper strip runs left to right, and long beats sit on one wide drawing that the camera travels along (the ridge, the tunnels, the front line) instead of a new picture per sentence. Something changes every 3-5 s: a tunnel draws on, a charge fills red, a figure nudges.
- **Hand-written numbers** (engine `letters`) on every key figure: 1955, 3:10, 1917, 19, 1,000,000 lb, 6, 5, 70 t, 200 t, 6,279, 122, 500 m, 130,000 km², $27.6B, 2028, 600.
- **Maps drawn in ink**, with paint for the thing that matters (the trench line from the Channel to Switzerland drawing itself; the five countries on Russia's border filling one by one).
- **Late draw-ons** (`late` / `drawAt`): the crater opens, the tunnel reaches the ridge, the drones appear over the minefield.
- **Paint never reads as blood.** Red is for charges and mines (things that wait), maps and the treaty seal. No red on people, wounds or casualties; casualty numbers are written in ink only.
- **Same paper, same ink, same calm.**
