# Short 23 "the Blue Hippo": gate 3 script review (fact check + cold listen)

Reviewer: fresh Fable 5.2 agent, 2026-11-23. Reviewed `narration.txt` (identical to `narration-v1.txt`) against `research/23-blue-hippo.md`, and re-opened the sources I could reach.

Not opened: the NYT 1931 pages (paywalled), Macy's Instagram story (read only as quoted by Fox 13, CBS New York, ABC7), YouTube search. The Nov 13 lineup release was not re-opened.

## Blocking (3)

**B1. "People phoned in to claim it. Nobody ever brought it back." Two different "it"s, and the first one says the wrong thing.**
The line before ends on "the reward", so the first "it" is heard as the reward and the second as the hippo. The only source (NYT 2017, Caron, opened) says: "Calls streamed in from people who claimed to have found the hippo, but they were simply seeking the reward money." So callers claimed to have found the balloon; "claim it" compresses that into something a listener can't unpack.
Replace with:
> People phoned in, saying they had found the hippo. Nobody ever brought it back.

**B2. Cover "Lost: one hippo" must not go out this week.**
Fiona, the Cincinnati Zoo hippo, died on Thu Nov 19 (confirmed: Scripps, Fox19, CBS New York). In a feed, with no narration, "Lost: one hippo" six days later reads as a joke about her, and Macy's own hippo windows already drew "too soon" posts. Use the research file's other cover:
> Last seen: flying

It is clearly about a balloon, adds a second puzzle to the title, and doesn't answer it. Cover paint blue, brand dot red.

**B3. Description needs one plain Fiona line, worded to what Macy's actually said.**
Macy's statement (Nov 21, as quoted by Fox 13, CBS New York and ABC7) says: it wanted "to mark Fiona's memory", it made a donation to the zoo with the zoo's blessing, and "Blue Hippo's handlers will wear a purple Fiona patch". None of the three reports says Macy's dedicated the balloon to her. Only the Journal-News paraphrases that the balloon "will be in Fiona's memory", and the research table repeats that. Don't write "dedicated" or "flies in her memory". Suggested line, no wit, placed above the sources:
> Macy's announced the Blue Hippo's return in August. After Fiona, the Cincinnati Zoo's hippo, died on November 19, Macy's said the balloon's handlers will wear a purple patch in her memory.

Keep Fiona out of the narration, title, cover, captions, tags and hashtags. The story is about a 1931 balloon; naming her in the video would either turn glib next to "burned" and "stays on its ropes", or look like trading on the news.

## Strong suggestions (in v2 below)

- **"hunting a hippo" to "looking for a hippo".** At that point the listener has not yet heard the word "balloon", and this week "hippo" means Fiona to a lot of people. "Hunting" is the 1931 headline's word and is accurate, but it is the one spot in the narration that can sound glib. The title already says "look for".
- **"the tiger" to "the tiger balloon".** "The tiger" and "the cat" arrive with "the" but were never introduced; only "giant balloons" was. Naming the first one as a balloon makes both clear on one listen, and stops "the cat ... hit a power line, and burned" being heard as an animal.

## Smaller notes

- The ending is close to Short 18's ("Today, they stay roped down the whole way"). Each Short stands alone, so it's fine, but it is the same closing idea twice in five Shorts.
- "it stays on its ropes" is fair: every report describes handlers (volunteers) holding the balloon. It is a statement of practice, not a prediction.
- "is flying" depends on Thursday. NYC grounds the giant balloons in high wind. Re-check the forecast and the news on Wednesday morning; fallback line: "Macy's is bringing a blue hippo back."
- No source gives the balloon's size or design. Journal-News calls it a replica of the 1931 one; nothing else confirms that. Don't call it a replica.
- Length: about 131 spoken words, slightly over the 110-130 target. Fine at Darren's pace (about 48-50 s).
- The 1931 NYT text is still unread first-hand. The radiogram-to-liners quote is carried by Good Housekeeping 2026 plus the 1931 headline ("Ocean Watch Asked by Macy's"); NYT 2017 carries "broadcast", "multiplied by four", and "soaring somewhere over the ocean". Good enough for the central link; open the Dec 2, 1931 article if anyone has access.

## Is the opening line literally true?

Yes. A department store (Macy's) did ask ships at sea (radiograms to the captains of transatlantic liners then at sea) to look out for a hippopotamus balloon the 1931 press called blue. The only softening is ours: "watch the sky" for "ocean watch".

## Cold listen (clarity check 1-5)

1. **Chain back:** Macy's used to release its parade balloons and pay a prize for returning them. People chased them: a ferry crew pulled the tiger from the river, the cat burned on a New Jersey power line. The blue hippo drifted past the Empire State Building and wasn't caught, so Macy's radioed ocean liners and raised the reward. It never came back. This year a blue hippo flies again, held down. The opening question is answered.
2. **Hook pay-offs:** "ships at sea" pays off in "So Macy's radioed the ocean liners"; "blue hippopotamus" in "But the blue hippo floated past..."; "department store" in "Why was Macy's...". All part of the answer.
3. **Pronouns:** "chased them" = the balloons, clear. "claim it" / "brought it back" = B1. "in its hundredth parade, Macy's" = Macy's, clear. "it stays on its ropes" = the new hippo, clear.
4. **Heard wrong:** "the tiger" and "the cat" (see suggestion); "hunting a hippo" (see suggestion).
5. **Repeated punchlines:** none inside the Short. No question pivots. No jargon ("ocean liners" is plain).

Story engine: stakes land by about 10 s (a prize for the public, a store that wants its balloon). Every beat joins with "so" or "but". The re-hook is "But the blue hippo ... kept going", around the middle. Three numbers (1931, four times, hundredth). Passes.

## Packaging

- Title "Why did Macy's ask ships to look for a blue hippo? 🦛": 52 characters, no numbers, doesn't answer the question, and names the color (so the cover paint is blue). Keep it.
- Cover: change to "Last seen: flying" (B2). Together with the title it makes the question bigger.
