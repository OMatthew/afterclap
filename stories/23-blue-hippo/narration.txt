In 1931, a department store asked ships at sea to watch the sky for a blue hippopotamus.

[[PAUSE 0.3]]

Back then, Macy's ended its Thanksgiving parade by letting the giant balloons go. Bring one back, and you won a prize.
So the city chased them. A ferry crew fished the tiger balloon out of the East River. The cat drifted to New Jersey, hit a power line, and burned.
But the blue hippo floated past the brand-new Empire State Building, and kept going.
So Macy's radioed the ocean liners, and made the reward four times bigger.
People phoned in, saying they had found the hippo. Nobody ever brought it back.

[[PAUSE 0.5]]

This year, in its hundredth parade, Macy's is bringing a blue hippo back.
This time, they're holding on to it.
