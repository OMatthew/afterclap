# Art prompt: Short 23 (the Blue Hippo)

ChatGPT in Chrome (chatgpt.com), one new chat, one image per message, default model. Composition guides: they get traced and re-inked. Reuses Short 18's letting-go and prize-man.

## Style (send once first)

I'm going to ask you for 7 drawings, one per message. Use this style for every one:
- A minimal pen sketch on plain white: about 25-45 lines in total.
- Black ink, one line weight, slightly wobbly, like a quick sketch in a notebook.
- Only outlines and a few inner lines. No background, no shading, no hatching, no fill, no color, no gray, no text, letters, numbers or logos.
- Lots of empty white paper. Subject centered, about 70% of the frame. Square image.
- Simple, friendly faces (dots for eyes, a short line for a mouth) are fine.
- The hippo balloon is an original design: a plain, round, friendly hippopotamus shape with small ears and stubby legs, like a 1930s parade balloon. Not any known cartoon or brand character. The tiger is a plain striped tiger; the cat a plain striped tabby.
- Not clip art, not vector: it should look drawn by a person.

## Items (file name: description)

1. liner-lookout: A 1930s ocean-liner officer in a peaked cap at the ship's rail, binoculars raised to the sky, face clearly visible (eyebrows up, curious). A lifebuoy on the rail and one funnel behind him.
2. ferry-tiger: Two ferry crewmen leaning over the side of a small 1930s ferry boat, hauling a soggy, half-deflated striped tiger balloon out of the river with a boat hook.
3. cat-wire: A saggy striped tabby cat balloon caught on electric power lines between two wooden poles, a small puff of smoke rising from it. No flames on any person.
4. empire-hippo: The round hippo balloon floating past the top of the Empire State Building (the spire and the stepped top only), the hippo bigger and closer than the tower.
5. radio-room: A 1930s ship's radio operator with big headphones, tapping a telegraph key, a round porthole behind him, face visible.
6. phone-caller: A man in a 1930s vest on a candlestick telephone, grinning, pointing up at nothing, as if saying "I've got it!".
7. handlers-hippo: The same round hippo balloon floating over a city street, held down by a long line of handlers with many ropes.
