# Art prompt: Short 16 (red and blue states)

Gemini in Chrome (gemini.google.com), one image per message in one new chat (Matthew, 2026-10-30: conserve ChatGPT usage). Composition guides only: they get traced and re-inked. Ask for minimal sketches from the start.

Paint: the narration names red and blue, so the paint takes those colors on the map states (red-and-blue tint), red elsewhere. Neutral: no party logos, no candidates' faces, no slogans.

## Style (send once first)

I'm going to ask you for 13 drawings, one per message. Use this style for every one:
- A minimal pen sketch on plain white: about 25-40 lines in total.
- Black ink, one line weight, slightly wobbly, like a quick sketch in a notebook.
- Only the main subject's outline and a few inner lines. No background, no shading, no hatching, no fill, no color, no gray, no text, letters, numbers or logos.
- Lots of empty white paper. Subject centered, about 70% of the frame. Square image.
- People seen from behind or from the side, or only as hands, with no detailed faces.
- Not clip art, not vector: it should look drawn by a person.
