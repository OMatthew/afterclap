# Art prompt: Short 14 (haunted houses)

ChatGPT in Chrome (chatgpt.com), one image per message in one new chat. Composition guides only: they get traced and re-inked. Lesson from Short 12: ChatGPT drifts toward detailed coloring-book pages; ask for minimal sketches from the start.

Groove check: no bats, no full moon, no cobwebs-and-tombstones clip art; the ghost is a plain sheet ghost, drawn simply. Red-not-blood: no paint on the sponge "brains" or the grape "eyes" (they would read as gore), on people, or on the ghost's body; paint goes on lit windows, a lantern, a ticket, a coin jar.

## Style (send once first)

I'm going to ask you for 14 drawings, one per message. Use this style for every one:
- A minimal pen sketch on plain white: about 25-40 lines in total.
- Black ink, one line weight, slightly wobbly, like a quick sketch in a notebook.
- Only the main subject's outline and a few inner lines. No background, no ground texture, no sky, no moon, no stars, no hatching, no shading, no fill, no color, no text or letters.
- Lots of empty white paper. The subject centered, about 70% of the frame. Square image.
- People seen from behind or from the side, or only as hands, with no detailed faces.
- Not clip art, not vector: it should look drawn by a person.
