Sent to ChatGPT in Chrome (chatgpt.com), one image per message in one chat. The drawings are composition guides: the template traces and re-inks them, so clear shapes matter more than polish.

Groove check on the picks: no Samhain/Celts, no bats, no witch hats, no "spooky" clip-art pumpkin patch. The coal/turnip/pumpkin beat is a plain row of three crossed-out things instead of a "myth busted" stamp. The ghost light is a small glow over reeds, not a ghost.

## Style (send once at the start, then one item per message)

I'm going to ask you for 16 drawings, one per message. Use this style for every one:
- Black ink line drawing on plain white, like a quick sketch with a fine pen in a notebook.
- One line weight, slightly wobbly.
- Sparse: draw only the lines that matter, with clear outer shapes.
- No shading, hatching, gray, fill or color. No text, letters, numbers, labels, border or background.
- One subject, centered, taking up about 70% of the frame, with plenty of white space around it.
- Square image.
- People are seen from behind or from the side, or only as hands, with no detailed faces.
- Not clip art and not vector: it should look drawn by a person.
Glows and flames: show them with a few short lines around the light, never with shading.
