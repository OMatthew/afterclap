# Art prompt: Short 18 (Macy's balloons)

ChatGPT in Chrome (chatgpt.com), one new chat, one image per message, default model setting (not Pro). Composition guides: they get traced and re-inked. First Short of the hook test: a face in the first scene, and big shapes the paint can fill (the balloons).

## Style (send once first)

I'm going to ask you for 9 drawings, one per message. Use this style for every one:
- A minimal pen sketch on plain white: about 25-45 lines in total.
- Black ink, one line weight, slightly wobbly, like a quick sketch in a notebook.
- Only outlines and a few inner lines. No background, no shading, no hatching, no fill, no color, no gray, no text, letters, numbers or logos.
- Lots of empty white paper. Subject centered, about 70% of the frame. Square image.
- Simple, friendly faces (dots for eyes, a short line for a mouth) are fine.
- Every balloon character is an original design, not any known cartoon character. The cat is a plain striped tabby (not black, no bow tie, no big white eyes); the tiger is a plain striped tiger.
- Not clip art, not vector: it should look drawn by a person.
