In 1932, a student pilot flew her plane straight into a giant balloon cat.
[[PAUSE 0.3]]
Why was a parade balloon up there at all? Because Macy's let them go on purpose.
After its Thanksgiving parade, Macy's released the giant balloons over New York. Whoever found one and brought it back won a prize. In 1928, that was a hundred dollars.
So people fought over them. On a rooftop in Queens, a tug-of-war tore a tiger balloon to pieces.
Even pilots joined the hunt. Macy's warned them: no prize for a balloon knocked down by a plane.
The student flew into the cat anyway. It wrapped around her wing, and the plane spun down toward the rooftops. Her instructor pulled it out just in time.
The next year, Macy's stopped letting the balloons go.
Today, they stay roped to their handlers the whole way. Nobody wants another flying cat.
