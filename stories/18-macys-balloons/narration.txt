In 1932, a student pilot flew her plane straight into a giant balloon cat.
[[PAUSE 0.3]]
Why was a parade balloon up there at all? Because Macy's let them go on purpose, as a prize hunt. In 1928, bringing one back won you a hundred dollars.
So people fought over them. On a rooftop in Queens, a tug-of-war tore a tiger balloon to pieces.
Even pilots joined the hunt. Macy's warned them: no prize for a balloon knocked down by a plane.
The student steered right into the cat. It wrapped around her wing, and the plane spun down toward the rooftops. Her instructor took over and pulled the plane out just above them.
By the next parade, Macy's had stopped letting the balloons go.
Today, they stay roped down the whole way. Nobody wants another flying cat.
