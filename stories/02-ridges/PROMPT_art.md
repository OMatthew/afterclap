# Art prompt: Short 02 (ridges)

Paste into a new chat in the ChatGPT Mac app (Work mode). The drawings are only composition guides: the template traces them and re-inks them by hand, so clear shapes matter more than polish.

---

Please make 14 separate images, one per file, and save each as a 1024x1024 PNG at the exact path given. Make them one at a time, and tell me when all 14 are saved.

Style for every image:
- Black ink line drawing on plain white, like a quick sketch with a fine pen in a notebook.
- One line weight, slightly wobbly.
- Sparse: draw only the lines that matter, with clear outer shapes.
- No shading, hatching, gray, fill or color. No text, letters, numbers, portraits, border or background.
- One subject, centered, taking up about 70% of the frame, with plenty of white space around it.
- Not clip art and not vector: it should look drawn by a person.
