Why do some cheese slices say "cheese product" on the wrapper? Because of a powder from overseas, some angry dairy farmers, and the FDA.

[[PAUSE 0.4]]

In America, some food names come with a recipe set by law. To be called "cheese food," a product can only use cheese plus things like milk, cream and whey.

[[PAUSE 0.4]]

Kraft Singles were sold as cheese food. But Kraft was adding milk protein concentrate, a powder made by filtering milk down to its protein. Much of it came from overseas, and American dairy farmers wanted something done about it.

[[PAUSE 0.4]]

That powder wasn't in the recipe. In 2002, FDA inspectors found it in Kraft's plants, so the slices couldn't be called cheese food anymore.

[[PAUSE 0.4]]

Kraft didn't take the powder out. It changed the name instead, to "cheese product." That name has no recipe at all.

[[PAUSE 0.5]]

So Kraft kept the powder, and dropped the word "food."
