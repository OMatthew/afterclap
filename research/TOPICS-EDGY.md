# Edgier topic seeds (2026-11-20)

Matthew: "pull no punches" (CHANNEL_RULES.md, Edgier topics). Seeds to research. Each needs a present-day angle, documented sources, and a YouTube coverage check (prefer what's not done to death, or a fresh angle on it). Leads, not facts: verify everything.

**Intelligence agencies and documented conspiracies**
- 1971: activists burgled an FBI office in Media, Pennsylvania, and exposed COINTELPRO; most were identified only in 2014. Present day: surveillance, leaks.
- Operation Northwoods (1962): US military plan for staged attacks to justify invading Cuba; rejected; declassified 1997.
- MKUltra: CIA mind-control experiments; most files destroyed in 1973; 1977 Senate hearings.
- The Church Committee (1975) and what it changed.
**Cartels and drugs**
- How the 1985 murder of DEA agent Kiki Camarena changed the drug war. Present day: fentanyl, cartels designated as terrorist organizations.
**Genocide and atrocity (with care)**
- Rwanda 1994: the radio station that helped organize a genocide (RTLM). Present day: incitement on social media.
**War and close calls**
- Petrov 1983; Arkhipov 1962; Able Archer 83; the 1995 Norwegian rocket.
- Mongols 1242 (long #2 candidate).
**Early humans and deep time**
- Interbreeding with Neanderthals and Denisovans: what's in our DNA today.
- Toba super-eruption and the "bottleneck" idea (contested).
- The Great Dying (end-Permian): the worst mass extinction, and what it says about warming.
**Dark mysteries with answers (or partial ones)**
- Dyatlov Pass: the 2021 slab-avalanche study.
- The Franklin expedition: lead, scurvy, cannibalism evidence, ships found 2014 and 2016.
- The whaleship Essex (inspired Moby-Dick): survivors who drew lots.
**Deadly voyages**
- Magellan's circumnavigation: roughly 18 of about 260 men came back.
- Shackleton's Endurance: crushed in the ice in 1915, found in 2022.
