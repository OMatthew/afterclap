# Short 23 research: the Blue Hippopotamus (sequel to 18)

Researched 2026-11-23 (research agent, web). Target: publish Wed 2026-11-25, the day before the 100th Macy's Thanksgiving Day Parade (Thu 2026-11-26).

**Verdict: PASS, with two wording fixes.** The hippo is real, was called the "Blue Hippo" in 1931 press, was hunted at sea and never recovered, and a Blue Hippo balloon flies in the 2026 parade. Fixes: (1) say the others were "accounted for", not "found" or "returned" (the cat burned, the dragon was torn up); (2) say it was "thought to be" over the Atlantic.

**Not read first-hand:** the NYT 1931 pages are blocked to our fetcher. Every 1931 quote below is as quoted by a later outlet (NYT 2017 piece, Good Housekeeping 2026, Untapped New York, NYPL). Someone with NYT access should open the Dec 2, 1931 article before voicing.

## Lore vs documented

**Documented (1931 press, via later quotation):**
- A hippopotamus balloon flew in the 1931 parade and was released. The NYT called it the "Blue Hippo" on Dec 2, 1931.
- It passed close to the Empire State Building on parade day (NYT Nov 27, 1931, via Untapped).
- Macy's searched by radio, sent radiograms to transatlantic liner captains, and quadrupled the reward.
- It was believed to be over the ocean. No recovery was ever reported.

**Macy's lore / framing (2026):**
- "Every balloon was accounted for except the Blue Hippopotamus." True in a loose sense for the big balloons we can trace (tiger recovered; cat burned; dragon shredded), but "accounted for" is doing work: at least two came back as wreckage. Coverage turns it into "every balloon was recovered" (Secret NYC) or "almost every balloon was returned" (Good Housekeeping). We have no 1931 source listing every balloon's fate (there was also a two-headed Martian, a turkey and Jerry the Pig), so **attribute nothing stronger than "the others turned up"** and show two or three.
- "Last seen soaring beyond the Empire State Building": matches the Nov 27 report, but it is the last *named place*, not proof of the last sighting; the Dec 2 search assumed it was at sea.
- "One of the earliest characters to soar" / "introduced in 1931": it appears to have flown only in 1931 (Fox 13; Retail Dive).
- The window story of the hippo's "adventures" is fiction.

**Contested / inconsistent:**
- Chamberlin's 1931 plane catch: Felix on a wing (NYT headline "Chamberlin in Plane Gives Foolish Feline a Lift"; Untapped; Transportation History) vs the dragon (Good Housekeeping). And Felix also "expired" on a New Jersey wire days later. Leave Chamberlin out.
- Direction of drift ("west toward Brooklyn"): wrong on its face. Leave out.
- Hippo reward in dollars: not stated anywhere.
- Lost "at sea" is a belief of the time, not an observation.

```
In 1931, a department store asked ships at sea to watch the sky for a blue hippopotamus.
[[PAUSE 0.3]]
```

Notes for gate 3:
- Stakes: the prize, and a store that wants its balloon back. But/so joins every beat. One question only. Re-hook at "But the blue hippo".
- "kept going" avoids asserting it reached the Atlantic; the hook and "ocean liners" carry the sea.
- "This year" dates the Short. Evergreen alternative: "In 2026, for its hundredth parade, Macy's flew a blue hippo again." (not true until Thursday; keep "is flying" for the Wednesday post).

## Risks

1. **Fiona.** A famous real hippo died on Nov 19; Macy's hippo windows opened the same day and drew "too soon" posts; the balloon now flies in her memory. A light Short about a "lost hippo" lands six days later. v1 keeps the wit on Macy's and the ships, never on the animal, and has no death language about the hippo. The lead should decide whether to add a description line, and should re-check the news on Wednesday morning.
2. **Lineup.** The Nov 13 lineup release does not list the hippo; no size, maker or position is public. If Macy's changes plans or weather grounds balloons, the last two lines are wrong. Re-check Wednesday.
3. **1931 text unread.** All 1931 quotes are second-hand. The "phoned in" line has one source.
4. **"Accounted for".** Don't say the others were all found or returned.
