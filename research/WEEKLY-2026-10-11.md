## How much to trust this

- Only three Shorts (01, 02, 04) got real feed reach. 03 (16 views) and 05 (88) never took off, so their high % viewed comes from a handful of viewers and means little.
- With 500–850 engaged views per Short, a gap of a few points in stayed-to-watch or % viewed is noise. Subscribers from Shorts total 10, so one more or one fewer moves a Short's share by 10 points.
- Why 03 and 05 stalled isn't knowable from this. Topic, timing and luck all fit. Watch whether 06 and 07 get a first-day burst.
- One week is directional at best; the plan's gate is day 30, and YouTube staff suggest ~90 days.
