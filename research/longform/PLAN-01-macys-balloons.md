**The story:** Macy's parade balloons. In 1932 a student pilot flew into a released Tom Cat balloon and nearly crashed, and Macy's stopped letting balloons go. In 1997 the Cat in the Hat balloon hit a lamppost and badly hurt a spectator, and New York set size, wind and handler rules for 1998. Two cats, 65 years apart, each changed the parade. Builds on Short 18 (research and about 10 drawings reusable).

**Shape (about 10:30):** cold open (the plane and the cat, then a second cat decades later) / 1924 live animals, balloons from 1927 (1927 vs 1928 release year to be checked against contemporary accounts) / letting them go: helium, reward tags, the chase / 1932 / the parade grows / 1997 / 1998 rules / back to the opening balloon.

**Title idea:** "How two cat balloons changed the Macy's parade" (no payoff). Thumbnail: one big inked cat balloon, a small biplane, one red accent, little or no text.

**Needs:** a 16:9 mode in the engine (today it is a vertical strip only); ~25-35 drawings (reuse Short 18's); one narration of ~1,500 words in the usual voice; the review gate as for Shorts.
